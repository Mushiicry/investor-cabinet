# Product Overview

## Product Name

Mushi Invest

Working title:
Investor Cabinet

---

## Product Category

Investor Operating System

---

## Product Purpose

Mushi Invest helps investors:

* understand portfolio structure
* control risk
* manage reserve capital
* maintain discipline
* survive market cycles
* make higher quality investment decisions

---

## Product Philosophy

Risk First.

Discipline First.

PnL Second.

The platform exists to help investors build and protect wealth through disciplined capital management.

---

## Product Goals

Provide a complete portfolio operating system.

Reduce emotional investing.

Increase clarity.

Improve capital allocation decisions.

Improve risk management.

Create a structured framework for long-term wealth building.

---

## What The Product Is Not

The product is not:

* a trading terminal
* a crypto casino
* a leverage platform
* a meme dashboard
* a signal service

---

## Core User

Private investor.
