# Risk System

## Purpose

The Risk System is the core competitive advantage of Mushi Invest.

Most portfolio trackers focus on:

* price
* charts
* PnL

Mushi Invest focuses on:

* survivability
* discipline
* capital protection
* risk visibility
* portfolio health

The Risk Layer is more important than the PnL Layer.

---

# Core Question

The Risk Layer exists to answer:

How safe is the portfolio?

---

# Risk Framework

The system evaluates risk through multiple dimensions:

* Health Factor
* Reserve Health
* Portfolio Concentration
* Asset Exposure
* Category Exposure
* Futures Exposure
* Liquidity Position
* Market Psychology

---

# Health Factor

## Purpose

Health Factor is the primary portfolio safety metric.

It summarizes the overall health of the portfolio.

The metric should be visible at all times.

---

## Interpretation

100 = ideal structure

80-99 = healthy

60-79 = caution

40-59 = elevated risk

0-39 = dangerous

---

## Inputs

Potential contributors:

* reserve allocation
* stablecoin allocation
* diversification
* position sizing
* futures exposure
* leverage exposure
* concentration risk
* market environment

---

# Reserve Layer

## Philosophy

Reserve is not unused capital.

Reserve is strategic flexibility.

Reserve allows:

* buying opportunities
* emotional stability
* protection during drawdowns
* avoidance of forced decisions

---

## Core Question

If the market falls 30% tomorrow, can the investor act confidently?

---

## Reserve Health

Reserve levels should be categorized.

Example:

Critical

Low

Healthy

Strong

Overfunded

# Exposure Layer

## Purpose

Measure risk concentration.

The system should identify:

* oversized positions
* oversized sectors
* excessive concentration

---

## Core Question

What can hurt the portfolio the most?

---

# Position Size Layer

The system should continuously monitor:

* largest position
* second largest position
* top 5 position share

Oversized positions increase risk.

---

# Futures Layer

## Philosophy

Futures are not the center of the portfolio.

Futures are tactical tools.

---

## Futures Risks

Monitor:

* leverage exposure
* liquidation risk
* portfolio dependency
* capital at risk

---

## Core Question

Are futures supporting the portfolio or threatening it?
